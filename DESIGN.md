# Design System: Shah

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Native Table"**

Shah should feel like an app that shipped with an iPhone running iOS 27. Two places make up the whole app. The table pages (home, Luaj, Ushtrime, lessons, the opening trainer) fill the screen with a lit wooden board in a dark room, and everything else floats over it as Liquid Glass: a status capsule and score card top left, a round glass menu button top right, the coach as one glass card, a slim accessory carrying the headlines and the move rail above a floating capsule tab bar. Away from the table the reading pages (Mësime, Progresi) sit in the same room: a still of the table in the chosen room behind the page, dimmed, with the lists as tinted glass (Luca, 2026-10-15: "one look everywhere"); only the 404 keeps the plain grouped background.

The system font and the iOS system colours carry the interface; one web font, Bodoni Moda, sets the titles (Luca, 2026-10-15: "a classic display typeface for titles"), the Didone of the Staunton years. Across the table sits a club of nine opponents, each an oil portrait of an invented person, chosen from a fanned deck of player cards (the signature moment) and seated in the score capsule, where they speak now and then. One tint blue does actions and selection; red, orange and green appear only as status (live, verdict grades, done marks). The board is an object, not a theme: maple and walnut squares, a mahogany frame with boxwood stringing, boxwood and ebony pieces, and a warm yellow on the last move, the same in both appearances. The broadcast world that came before this one (stone and ink bars, poster Mona Sans, the orange pointer) is gone; Luca replaced it on 2026-10-13 with "make now everything more like iOS 27 theme".

**Key Characteristics:**
- The lit table fills the screen on table pages; controls float over it as glass and never sit beside it in a sidebar.
- Liquid Glass is one recipe: tinted base, top sheen, lit rim, hairline, lifting shadow, heavy blur. The learner sets its tint from clear to tinted.
- Table pages always use the dark token set; list pages follow the system appearance.
- The system font for everything read; Bodoni Moda for titles, names and figures at display sizes.
- Capsules for every control, continuous large radii for cards, lists and sheets.
- One tint for action, status colours only for status, yellow only for the last move, brass only for what was earned.

## Colors

The iOS system palette in light and dark, a dark glass for the table, and the warm materials of a wooden set under a lamp.

### Primary
- **System Blue** (tint; tint-dark #0a84ff on dark): the one action and selection colour. Checkmarks, the slider track, the current-move cap on the rail, the review graph's now-line, focus rings (75% mix), text selection (32% mix), the caret. The 3D board reads it live from `--pointer` (an alias of `--tint`) for move marks, chosen square, arrows and their discs.
- **Filled Blue** (tint-fill): any blue fill that carries white text (solid buttons, the on cue, lesson step tiles, the board plate, the skip link, the study tag). A shade deeper than the system tint so white text passes AA.
- **Blue Ink** (tint-ink; tint-ink-dark #409cff): blue text and icons: quiet buttons, links, the current tab, guide times.

### Secondary: status
- **Red** (red, red-fill, red-ink): the Live tag (red-fill, white text, a dot in currentColor), blunders (grade pill, informant symbol, rail tick, graph marks), the low clock, the reset row. red-fill exists for the same AA reason as tint-fill.
- **Orange** (orange, orange-ink): inaccuracies and mistakes, the coach's warning line.
- **Green** (green, green-ink): best and good moves, the side to move on the score card (a dot with a 3px 28% halo), done marks, wins.

### Tertiary: the last move
- **Last-Move Yellow** (last): the warm highlight chess players expect, in both appearances: 0.4 opacity on the flat board, 0.26 and not tone mapped in 3D.

### Neutral
- **Grouped Background, Cell, Pressed Cell** (bg, cell, cell-press; bg-dark and cell-dark in dark, cell-press #2c2c2e): the page, the list groups, the pressed row.
- **Labels** (label, label-2, label-3): primary, secondary and tertiary text. Light label-2 is rgb(60 60 67 / 0.78), darker than iOS ships, so secondary text clears 4.5:1 on the grouped background. Dark and table pages use rgb(235 235 245 / 0.62) and 0.66 respectively.
- **Separators and Fills** (sep, fill, fill-2, seg-thumb): hairlines at 0.5px, the gray capsule fills, the segmented track and thumb.
- **Glass** (glass, glass-stage): the floating surfaces. Canonical values are expressions of `--glass-level` (default 0.5): light rgb(248 248 250 / 0.45 + level x 0.5), dark rgb(38 38 42 / 0.36 + level x 0.52), table rgb(30 32 34 / 0.34 + level x 0.52); the frontmatter records the default.

### The table and the set
- **The room under the canvas:** while the table loads, and as the whole picture for the flat board, the stage shows the chosen room's still at 36px wide, inlined in the CSS (about 200 bytes each), so it paints with the page; scaled up it is as soft as the lens's blur, and the 3D room takes over without a jump (it was a green felt pool, which read as an empty screen on a slow connection). A vignette to 45% black at the corners and a 4rem fade into the page at its foot. The 3D felt is a 160-unit plane of noise cloth (repeat 90).
- **Studio Dark** (stage-ground): the 3D clear colour and fog colour.
- **Maple and Walnut** (sq-light, sq-dark): the flat squares. The 3D squares are painted per veneer leaf, maple hsl(34 50% 66%) and walnut hsl(23 44% 37%), each leaf shifted a shade, so the near-white boxwood pieces stand off the maple and the ebony off the walnut.
- **Mahogany, Edge, Boxwood Stringing, Coordinate Inlay** (mahogany, mahogany-edge, boxwood-stringing, coord-inlay): the frame, its stroke, the two pale lines, the inlaid coordinates (rgba(236, 214, 170, 0.9) in 3D).
- **Boxwood and Ebony** (boxwood, ebony): the 3D pieces. Flat pieces use piece-fill-w with piece-edge-w and piece-fill-b with piece-edge-b.

### Brass: what was earned
- **Brass** (brass #d9b26f, brass-ink #e2c48c; brass-light-ink #8a5a22 in the day room): the metal of the board's corner caps, used for achievement only: the course path walked so far, done marks, chapter numerals, the course progress bars (a #c99a52 to #ecd29c gradient), wins in charts and the ladder, the medals. Never an action, never a selection.
- **Results** (win #d9b26f, draw #b9b2a6, loss #e0675c; a step deeper in the day room): the accuracy chart's dots and the ladder's bars, always with the numbers or a key beside them.
- **Player card** (card-ground #1b130e, card-ink #f6efe4, level in #e6c891): the deck's cards keep their own dark ground in every room, like a printed card.
- **Diagram** (diagram-light #ead3a6, diagram-dark #8d5338, a #3b1a0e frame with a pale stringing ring): the small boards printed in the course and on the dashboard.

### Named Rules
**The One Tint Rule.** Blue means "you can act here" or "this is chosen". Status colours never stand in for it, and it never decorates.

**The Table Is A Place Rule.** Table pages use the dark token set whatever the system appearance; the room decides, not the page: the bright day room (Dritë dite) takes the light glass and dark ink (`data-world-tone="light"` on the root, set before paint from `shah.world`). Only the list pages follow the system light and dark.

**The Earned Brass Rule.** Brass appears only where the progress shows something done. A medal is struck only when its condition is met by the stored progress; nothing is estimated or given.

**The White-On-Fill Rule.** White text sits only on tint-fill or red-fill, never on the brighter system tint or red.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bodoni Moda (Indestructible Type, SIL OFL 1.1), self-hosted from `public/fonts/` as two variable WOFF2 files (roman 46 KB, italic 54 KB; Latin subset, which holds ë and ç; weight 400-900 and optical size 6-96 axes), the roman preloaded, `font-display: swap`. Fallbacks: Bodoni 72, Didot, Bodoni MT, Georgia.
**Body Font:** the system font (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, SF Pro Text, system-ui, Segoe UI Variable Text, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Label/Mono Font:** none; clocks, counts and move numbers use tabular figures

**Character:** a Didone at display sizes over the system sans, the way a luxury label sets its name over plain print. The page sets body at 1.0625rem with -0.012em tracking, as iOS does. The Didone's thin hairlines are only ever set large (from Title 3 up), where they hold over the room.

### Hierarchy
- **Wordmark** (Bodoni Moda 600, clamp(5.5rem, 2rem + 18vw, 10.5rem), 0.9, -0.035em): "Shah" on home at poster size, white at 0.9 over the table. The intro mark is the same at clamp(4.5rem, 2rem + 12vw, 8rem); the navigation mark is Bodoni 700 at Title 3 (Title 2 on desktop).
- **Display Title** (Bodoni 600, clamp(3.25rem, 2.25rem + 4.5vw, 6rem), 0.98, -0.025em): the reading pages' titles (Mësime, Progresi), lifted off the room by a soft shade.
- **Figures** (Bodoni 600, clamp(2.25rem, 1.5rem + 3.5vw, 3.25rem), tabular): the three numbers at the head of Progresi; the course count at 1.625rem.
- **Title 1** (Bodoni 600, 1.75rem; 2rem on desktop): the move in the coach card, the new-game sheet title, chapter titles, the dashboard's opponent (1.625rem), the deck's names (1.75rem). Non-move coach states drop to Title 2 and wrap.
- **Title 2** (Bodoni 600, 1.375rem): group headers on the reading pages.
- **Title 3** (Bodoni 600, 1.25rem): lesson titles on the path, the ladder's names, the first-run answers.
- **Chapter numeral** (Bodoni 500, 3.25rem, roman, brass-ink): I to V before each chapter; upright, because the italic capitals read as slashes at this size.
- **Kicker** (system 600, 0.75rem, uppercase, 0.05-0.06em tracking, label-2): the small label above a Didone title ("Kapitulli II", "Kundërshtari yt", "Lojëra"), as a book sets a part title.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.4): list rows, buttons (600), guide rows.
- **Subhead** (0.9375rem): coach reasons (1.38, 60ch; callout 1rem on desktop), cues (600), segmented cells, ledes (46ch).
- **Footnote** (0.8125rem, 400 to 700): status text, score rows, grade pills, secondary row lines, group notes.
- **Caption / Caption 2** (0.75rem 700; 0.6875rem 600): the Live tag and lesson meta; tab labels.

### Named Rules
**The Text Styles Rule.** Read text keeps to the iOS text-style ladder (--t-large to --t-cap2). The Didone takes the display sizes above it. Uppercase appears only as the kicker over a Didone title, never as a section header or on a button.

**The Larger Text Rule.** Cilësimet > "Tekst më i madh" (`shah.text`, applied in the head before paint as `html[data-text='large']`) raises every read size one step, as iOS's first larger-text step does: body 19px, callout 18, subhead 17, footnote 15, caption 14, caption2 13. Titles and spacing keep their size. The fixed bars keep their size too, as an iPhone's status and tab bars do: the score capsules, the status capsule, the move strip, the tab bar, the navigation bar and the page tabs. Every one-screen page still fits at 320 x 568 with it on.

**The Figurine Rule.** Moves are written as figurine plus square (piece silhouette inline at 0.95em, 0.82em in the coach headline, then the square in 600 tabular figures), never as letter notation.

## Layout

Phone first. A table page is a stage of 100svh (min 36rem) with every glass surface absolutely positioned inside it at 0.75rem from the edges. On phones the navigation bar is removed from table pages and the motion switch moves into the tools menu. From the bottom up: the floating tab bar (3.875rem, a gap of max(safe-area, 0.75rem)) and the coach card just above it, centred at min(100% - 1.5rem, 34rem) (30rem for the tab bar). `--chrome-bottom` names the band the tab bar holds. Below 1024px the coach card is compact and keeps to its band under the still board (see The Still Board Rule) (Luca, 2026-10-13: it "takes like half the screen"): padding 0.6875 / 0.875 / 0.75rem, radius 1.5rem, the move at Title 2 and other states at Title 3, footnote pill and warning, and when a primary cue shows, the secondary cues become 44px round symbol buttons beside it (their text stays in the DOM as the accessible name). The accessory is hidden on phones; it only repeated the coach and general tips. Together the card and tab bar hold about a third of a 390 x 844 screen. At the top of a phone (Luca: "remove the 1 from top and make it more compact"), the status capsule is visually hidden wherever a score card exists (it stays for screen readers; the opponent's turn dot breathes while the engine thinks), and the score card becomes one 2.25rem capsule holding both players side by side (the opponent's label truncates, the learner's never does), with a 2.25rem menu button whose ::after keeps a 44px hit area; the open menu tightens to 2.75rem rows at callout size. On the play page a glass move strip sits under it (chess.com's idiom): every move in one line, numbers in label-3, the move on the board in a lighter capsule, sliding smoothly (instantly with motion off) to keep that move in view, the oldest moves fading at the leading edge; a tap opens the move. Insets: 3.5rem, 5.75rem with the strip. The board runs edge to edge on phones (Luca: "make the board bigger on phone"): side insets 0; the 3D board in the seat or overhead view frames the playing field (half + 0.04) across the screen and lets the frame's sides fall off it, the flat board crops its viewBox to 0 -44 800 888; on both the rank numbers move onto the left-hand file's squares (top-left, 700, in the other wood's colour) and the frame's own are not drawn. On a 390px screen the playing field grows by about 13%.

The board frames itself inside the stage insets `--inset-top / bottom / left / right` (phone 7.25 / 19 / 0.5 / 0.5rem; home 16 / 17rem). They must stay plain lengths, never calc(): the 3D board reads them with parseFloat.

From 1024px the iPad idiom takes over: the tab bar hides and the tabs float as one glass capsule in the middle of a 4.25rem navigation bar, fixed over the table on table pages; gutter 2rem. The status capsule and score card (21rem) sit at the top of a left column at 1.5rem, the coach card below them (21rem, 25rem from 1400px), the last moves as a glass card at the column foot, tools as a row of round glass buttons top right, the accessory spanning the foot. Insets become 5.5 / 5 / 24 (28 from 1400px) / 4.75rem. Home puts its dashboard on the right at 27rem.

**The One Screen Rule** (Luca, 2026-10-16: "everything inside a page, not to scroll … except the settings … like a real app"). No page scrolls; only the settings (`/progresi/cilesimet/`, the App prop `scroll`, `data-scroll` on the root) do. Without `data-scroll` the root and body are locked at 100% with no overscroll. What used to run below a page now lives in one of two places:
- **A table's extras rise as a sheet** (`src/scripts/panel.ts`): the scoresheet, the puzzle themes, the games to review and the opening lines (see Sheets).
- **A reading page pages sideways** (`src/scripts/pager.ts`): a `.screen` of `100dvh - --mast-h` (max 46rem, 72rem from 1024px; the tab bar's band kept free below it). It holds the large title with a one-line count, a row of tabs, and a scroll-snapped strip of pages, each exactly as tall as what is left.
  - **The tabs** are 44px capsules (padding clamp 0.5–0.875rem; caption size under 360px wide, so Progresi's four fit a 320px phone as one segmented control).
  - **A longer row of tabs** (the course's six) slides sideways and keeps the chosen one in view.
- **Inside a page** nothing scrolls either: the rows share the height, and a short phone lets the secondary line under each title go (`max-height` 720 and 640px) rather than cut anything off.

The room still stands behind them (`.room-bg`, fixed, cover, dimmed by a 0.78 / 0.46 / 0.62 gradient of rgb(12 8 6), or a white wash in the day room), the cards in room glass. The settings page keeps the long column: group headers 2.25rem above their group. Spacing steps: 0.375 / 0.625 / 1 / 1.5 / 2.25 / 3rem. Breakpoints: 640px (the sheet centres), 768px (larger large title), 1024px (desktop shell), 1400px (wider column). Tap targets are at least 44px; primary buttons 50px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is Liquid Glass over the table and flat cells on the list pages. Every floating surface uses one recipe; list groups have no shadow and separate from the grouped background by colour alone. The navigation bar is no bar: a scroll-edge layer under it (80% bg fading out, blur 10px, masked to transparent 1.25rem below) softens what scrolls beneath.

### Shadow Vocabulary

**The Frame Budget.** The opening must run smooth on a phone (Luca, 2026-10-15: "the opening animation with the board and pieces is laggy"). Measured at 390 x 844 at 2x through the opening: 1.67 million triangles and 27.5 ms a frame with two 0.3 s stalls before; about 0.45 million and 18 ms with none after. The rules that got it there: the shadow map draws a coarse stand-in of each piece, never the fine one (soft VSM shadows also draw every receiver: the fine piece skips its turn in the map); the pieces are made at about a third of their first triangle count; every program is compiled before the first frame that needs it, for the screen and for the lens's target alike (the whole set is made and drawn once at load, and one contact ring is always drawn out of sight); the canvas is never resized to the size it already has (that reallocates its buffers). `__board.exp({ noShadows, noDof, noAdapt, noStill })` in development switches costs off to measure them.

**The Still Table.** Pieces in play move at the screen's own rate (Luca, 2026-10-16: "make the pieces move smoother when playing"). Drawing the whole lacquered room for every frame of a move cost 35 to 54 ms of GPU on a 390 x 844 phone at 2x, so a piece moved at 20 to 30 frames a second with jumps of 67 to 133 ms. While a game is played the camera stands still (The Still Board Rule), so `still.ts` draws the room, the board and every piece at rest once into a picture that keeps its depth, and each frame lays that picture back and draws only the pieces in motion over it, with their contact rings, their reflections and their own shadow (the lamp's map holds only them while they move, redrawn every other frame, and two see-through catchers on the board's face and the table take it). A moving frame costs 7 to 15 ms; the flights run at 16.7 ms a frame. The picture is drawn as the screen is (each material tone maps and encodes its own colour, so the see-through parts blend as they do on the screen; a linear picture made every contact shadow a shade darker), and a moving frame matches a full one but for the moving piece. It is made while the table is quiet: the bot's during its think (`prepare()`), the learner's as the piece is picked up, which is the one frame that pays for it. The last move's squares and the threat rings wait for the piece to land; the move's dots and the keyboard ring are drawn over the picture, so they go the moment the move is made. A new view (a flip, another angle, a new size) throws the picture away; the home screen's lens never uses one. A move flies on a sine ease (its fastest point half again the average speed; the cubic it replaced darted at three times it) and an arc of 0.32 + 0.07 a square, and is set down where it lands: the old push down drove it 4% of a square into the lacquer. The canvas only lowers its resolution when nothing moves, and the sounds' audio is woken while the page is idle (made at the first touch it cost 60 ms).

### Board
**The Light Board** ("Tabela e lehtë", `board3d.ts`): a weak phone draws the table with fewer effects: no lens blur on home, no reflections in the lacquer, a 512px shadow map, no swing round the table on home, and a resolution of 1. It is chosen in Cilësimet (`shah.light` on / off), or comes on by itself: a phone that reports 2 GB or less, a software or budget GPU (SwiftShader, llvmpipe, older Mali, Adreno and PowerVR), or a board still too slow (a third of its frames over 25ms, judged every 45 frames or 3 seconds) after the resolution has stepped down to 1; then it is remembered (`shah.light.auto`). Quality changes wait while pieces move, not while home's camera swings, so the home screen adapts too. Measured on a software GPU: home 1 frame a second full, 13 light.

The board is always the 3D table on every page (Luca, 2026-10-13: "i want the board to be always 3d"); there is no switch. Puzzles, lessons and the trainer put unused pieces away when they jump to a position (keepTaken: false), while a game keeps what was taken lying beside the board. The flat board remains only as the fallback where WebGL fails: an SVG with the same frame, stringing, maple and walnut and inlaid coordinates. Marks: last move in last-move yellow, check in the status red (0.5 on the flat board; the 3D check glow takes `--red` too), chosen tint at 0.3, focus a 7px tint ring, move dots tint at 0.85, captures as tint rings, keyboard cursor a dashed black square, arrows tint at 0.88 (quiet 0.66) with a white number in the system font on a tint disc. 3D pieces settle on springs (rest k170 c19, carry k560 c42, tilt k210 c17); flat pieces slide 240ms ease-out. Arrows draw on from the tail.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** fill table pages with the lit board and float every control over it as Liquid Glass by the shared recipe.
- **Do** use the dark token set on every table page, whatever the system appearance.
- **Do** use the system font and the iOS text-style ladder for everything read, and Bodoni Moda for titles, names and figures from Title 3 up.
- **Do** put white text only on tint-fill (#0066d6) or red-fill (#d70015), and keep light label-2 at rgb(60 60 67 / 0.78).
- **Do** use the tint for action and selection, red, orange and green only for status, and yellow only for the last move.
- **Do** keep `--inset-*` on the stage as plain lengths; the 3D board parses them with parseFloat.
- **Do** honour Reduce Transparency (solid glass) and Increase Contrast (drawn rim), and make the Stop-animations switch and reduced motion zero transitions with 0s, not 0.01ms.
- **Do** keep every light out of the board: the lacquered parts go through `noGlare()`, so neither the lamp nor a light in the room's photograph ever shines in it (check the low home view in each room).

### Don't:
- **Don't** build the flat green-and-cream board widget with a grey eval bar and a sidebar of tabs.
- **Don't** add web-dashboard chrome: cards in grids, hairline-bordered panels, uppercase section headers. (The medal grid is a case of objects, not a grid of cards.)
- **Don't** give the table a light theme: felt, wood and pieces keep their tones in both appearances.
- **Don't** load a second web font or set the Didone small; don't set text as an icon glyph: icons are Phosphor SVG, and list symbols are CSS masks.
- **Don't** let the opponent chatter: one line at a time, four half-moves apart, never over the board.
- **Don't** start a second animation clock: presses and scrolling use CSS and the Web Animations API; anything timed in script goes through GSAP's ticker.
- **Don't** strike a medal or gild a step the stored progress does not show.
- **Don't** put a solid bar under the navigation; the scroll edge is a fading blur.
